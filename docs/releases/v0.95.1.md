# Tome v0.95.1

The first 0.95 release. The `v0.95.0` tag was created, but its build failed a test, so no 0.95.0 image or release was published. 0.95.1 is that release, plus the fixes below.

## Before you upgrade

- **Back up `tome.db`.** This release adds migrations for the format of each read (ebook, audiobook or print) and for audiobook page counts. Tome backs up the database before migrating, but keep your own copy too.

## What's new

- **Themes.** **Settings → Theme** adds the museum themes shared with Aurearia, Domus and Watch Tracker: Aurea, Parchment, British Museum, Louvre, Capitoline, Byzantine and Modern Greek. Each one comes with Cinzel headings. Tome's own Light and Dark, and Match device, are unchanged.
- **Sync has its own page.** The reading apps (Audiobookshelf and Grimmory) moved out of Settings to **More → Sync** (`/sync`): each app's last sync, **Sync now**, and the matched books.
- **Select without a select mode.** Point at a book in the Library, a shelf or Read Next and a circle appears; click it to select the book. On a phone the circle is always shown. Once a book is selected, clicking or tapping any other book adds it to the selection, and the action bar appears. **Done** clears the selection. The **Select** buttons are gone.
- **Bulk actions in the library.** Tag, shelve or delete many books at once, or select every book that matches the filters.
- **Reading report.** A yearly report with covers, a summary, sorting and paging, which you can export as Markdown or CSV.
- **Reading Rhythm and Year in review.** A calendar heatmap and reading pace on the Stats page, plus a year-in-review page.
- **Audiobooks count.** Audiobooks get a page count, so listening is logged like reading, and each read records its format.
- **MCP server.** Manage the backlog and see reading progress from an MCP client.

## Fixes

- **Page titles on phones.** Page headers, including the Library's, fit the title and its buttons on one line at phone width, or put the buttons under the title. Before, the wider museum-theme headings ran into them.

- **ISBN look-up.** A slow Open Library is retried once instead of failing on the first try. When it can't be reached, the error says so.
- **Muted text, tints and soft borders now show.** Some faded colours compiled to no CSS and rendered at full strength or not at all. A test now catches it.
- **Background sync no longer logs page-refresh errors.**
